INTRODUCTION

From May to December 2026, I had the opportunity to work with Kenna, a marketing solutions company located in Mississauga, Ontario. I supported the collection, validation, reconciliation, and quality assurance of data from retail and distributor customers across Canada. The role gave me hands-on experience working with large datasets while also strengthening my communication, problem-solving, and organizational skills.

My time at Kenna helped me better understand how accurate and reliable data supports business operations, while giving me valuable experience working in a professional and collaborative environment.

01

INFORMATION ABOUT THE EMPLOYER

Kenna connects data, technology and customer experience.

01 / COMPANY

Who is Kenna?

Kenna is a marketing solutions company that works at the intersection of marketing, technology, and data. Rather than focusing on a single type of service, Kenna supports clients through a mix of digital solutions.

It was very interesting to see how important data is to Kennas day-to-day work. A lot of the company relies on having accurate data, and my team was responsible for helping process and check that information. Working here gave me a better understanding of how data collection and quality assurance can directly support larger marketing and customer experience initiatives.

I also found Kenna to have a suprisingly welcoming workplace culture. As a co-op student, I was given just as many responsibilities as the full-time staff, while being surrounded by people who were willing to support my learning.

02 / COMPUTING

Where computing fits

This role was closely related to computer science because a large part of the work involved understanding how data is stored, processed, and connected across different systems. Working with SQL and PostgreSQL gave me hands-on experience with databases and showed me how important it is to structure and validate data properly so that other applications and reports can use it reliably.

It also involved a lot of problem solving, which is a big part of computer science. When data did not match or something looked incorrect, I had to investigate the issue, trace where the problem came from, and figure out the best way to fix it. This helped me apply the same logical thinking I use in programming to real business and data problems.

GOALS & LEARNING OUTCOMES

What I wanted to learn.

GOAL 01COMPLETED

Oral Communication

Goal: As a co-op student, I want to improve my oral communication skills by becoming more comfortable speaking with coworkers, asking clear questions, and giving updates on my assigned tasks. In my role as a Data Collection and Integration Assistant at Kenna, I will work on communicating my progress, challenges, and any support I need in a clear and professional way. Another opportunity for me to better my oral communication is through the outgoing calls I will have to make to retailers/growers. This will help me contribute more effectively to the team and build confidence in a workplace environment.

Action Plan: I will track the calls I make to retailers and growers and make short scripts to help guide the conversation. This will help me stay organized, keep the calls focused, and make sure I gather the information needed. I will work on asking clearer questions by making sure I understand the issue before asking for help. I will also try to use the correct terms and reference the correct tools such as tables, servers and any information in AgDeliver when communicating with my team. I will also try to speak up more during team connects and communicate more with colleagues to become more comfortable communicating in the workplace.

Measure of Success: I will measure my success by how comfortable I've become when making calls to retailers and growers, while still keeping confidentiality in mind. A sign of improvement would be gathering the needed information with ease and keeping calls short, clear, and to the point. I will also measure my success based on the feedback I receive from Jen in my mid and final evaluation, especially regarding my communication, professionalism, and ability to clearly explain my progress and challenges.

Reflection:

During this work term, I made progress toward improving my oral communication skills. I became more comfortable speaking with coworkers, asking questions when I needed clarification, and giving updates on my work. Making calls to retailers and growers also helped me become more confident speaking professionally and keeping conversations clear and focused.

GOAL 02COMPLETED

Critical & Creative Thinking

Goal: As a co-op student, I want to improve my critical and creative thinking skills by analysing information carefully, and look for patterns or issues in the data I work with. In my role as a Data Collection and Integration Assistant at Kenna, I will practice reviewing data, identifying possible errors or gaps, and thinking through different ways to solve problems. This will help me become more confident in making informed decisions and contributing useful insights to the team.

Action Plan: I will improve my inquiry and analysis skills by learning how to use SQL terms more effectively when searching for threshold-related information. I will also focus on understanding which tables are most relevant for different tasks so I can find the correct information more efficiently. For linking tasks, I will work on choosing the correct search parameters before completing a link. This will help me analyze the available information more carefully and make better decisions when matching records.

Measure of Success: I will measure my success by the accuracy of my work, especially when completing thresholds and linking tasks. A sign of improvement would be completing tasks with fewer errors and needing less correction from my team. I will also measure my success by the number of thresholds and links I complete. As I become faster and more confident with SQL searches, table selection, and search parameters, I should be able to complete a larger quantity of work while still maintaining high accuracy.

Reflection: During this work term, I improved my critical thinking and analysis skills by working with data, SQL queries, and linking tasks. I became better at identifying the correct tables and search parameters to find the information I needed. I also learned to slow down and review the available information before making decisions, which helped reduce errors. Over time, I became more accurate and confident in completing threshold and linking tasks with less support from my team.

GOAL 03COMPLETED

Technical Skills

Goal: As a co-op student, I want to improve my SQL skills and become more confident using databases in a workplace setting. In my role as a Data Collection and Integration Assistant at Kenna, I will work on completing my assigned tasks early and efficiently so I can use extra time to take on additional SQL-related tasks. This will help me build stronger technical skills while also improving my time management and independence.

Action Plan: I will work on completing my assigned tasks early and efficiently so I can create more time to focus on SQL-related work. By managing my time well, I can take on additional technical tasks. such as threshold analysis with Alex. This will give me more practice working with data. I could also possibly support quality assurance for new code releases in the test environment, which will help me understand how database changes and query results connect to the larger workflow.

Measure of Success: I will measure my success by whether my query results are accurate and valid. This includes making sure the threshold percentages from any threshold analysis I complete are correct and useful for the team. I will also measure my success by how quickly I am able to put together a query. As I improve, I should be able to understand the relationships between tables more clearly and build queries with more confidence and less support.

Reflection: Throughout my co-op, I became more comfortable using SQL and working with databases in a professional environment. I improved at understanding relationships between tables, building queries, and checking that my results were accurate. As I became more efficient with my regular tasks, I was able to spend more time on technical work and strengthen my SQL skills. This experience helped me become more independent and showed me how database work connects to larger workflows within a company.
