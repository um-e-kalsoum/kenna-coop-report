INTRODUCTION

From May to December 2026, I have the opportunity to work with Kenna, a marketing solutions company located in Mississauga, Ontario. I supported the collection, validation, reconciliation, and quality assurance of data from retail and distributor customers across Canada. The role gave me hands-on experience working with large datasets while also strengthening my communication, problem-solving, and organizational skills.

My time at Kenna helped me better understand how accurate and reliable data supports business operations, while giving me valuable experience working in a professional and collaborative environment.

01

INFORMATION ABOUT THE EMPLOYER

Kenna connects data, technology and customer experience.

01 / COMPANY

Who is Kenna?

Kenna is a marketing solutions company that works at the intersection of marketing, technology, and data. Rather than focusing on a single type of service, Kenna supports clients through a mix of digital solutions.

It was very interesting to see how important data is to Kennas day-to-day work. A lot of the company relies on having accurate data, and my team was responsible for helping process and check that information. Working here gave me a better understanding of how data collection and quality assurance can directly support larger marketing and customer experience initiatives.

I also found Kenna to have a suprisingly welcoming workplace culture. As a co-op student, I was given just as many responsibilities as the full-time staff, while being surrounded by people who were willing to support my learning.

02 / COMPUTING

Where computing fits

This role was closely related to computer science because a large part of the work involved understanding how data is stored, processed, and connected across different systems. Working with SQL and PostgreSQL gave me hands-on experience with databases and showed me how important it is to structure and validate data properly so that other applications and reports can use it reliably.

It also involved a lot of problem solving, which is a big part of computer science. When data did not match or something looked incorrect, I had to investigate the issue, trace where the problem came from, and figure out the best way to fix it. This helped me apply the same logical thinking I use in programming to real business and data problems.

Conclusions

The End



During Summer 2026 I had the opportunity to work at Kenna as a Data Collection & Integration Assistant Co-op. Over the course of my work term, I was able to learn more about how data supports different parts of a business and gain experience working in a professional environment. Looking back, I am very glad I had the chance to be part of the team and take on responsibilities that helped me grow throughout the term.

One of the biggest areas I improved in was working with data and databases. I became more comfortable using tools such as Excel, SQL, and PostgreSQL, while also learning how to investigate discrepancies, validate information, and make sure data was accurate before it was used elsewhere. I also developed stronger problem-solving skills because many tasks required me to look closely at information, identify what was wrong, and figure out the best way to resolve it.

This role also helped me become much more confident with communication. Speaking with retailers and customers, asking questions, and working with my team helped me improve both my written and verbal communication. I became more comfortable handling tasks independently while also learning when it was important to ask for help or clarification.

One of my favourite parts of the experience was the people at Kenna. The other co-op students made lunches and breaks something to look forward to, and the full-time staff were always friendly and supportive. The office events and Thursday socials also made the workplace feel more welcoming and gave everyone a chance to connect outside of regular work.

Overall, I am leaving this summer with stronger technical skills, better communication and problem-solving abilities, and a much better understanding of how data is used in a real workplace. Most importantly, this co-op gave me valuable experience that I can carry into future computer science and software-related roles, along with great memories and connections from my time as a Kennadian.
